# Daily Gary episodes: legends list

One new episode per day, taken from the top of **Up next**. When an episode ships, move it to **Done** with
the date and video path. Facts in each script must be checked with a web search before narrating.

Format for every episode (same as AB de Villiers / Virat Kohli):
title card, then #5 → #1 of the legend's famous shots, skills or moments (the legend does it perfectly,
then "GARY'S TURN" and Gary fails comically, and the "TIMES GARY FAILED" counter ticks up), then a short outro gag.
About 90–100 s of narration, Kokoro `bm_george` voice, shared high-energy soundtrack.
